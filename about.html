Our Story

Where it all began.

Vertex Education was born from a simple but powerful belief — that every student in Chandkheda deserves the same quality of coaching available in India's biggest cities. In 2014, we opened with a handful of students, a passionate team, and a commitment to genuine teaching.

Today, 500+ students later, that belief is unchanged. We've grown into Ahmedabad's most trusted coaching institute — but our batches stay small, our teaching stays personal, and our dedication to every student stays absolute.

2014
The Beginning
Opened in Chandkheda with a small team and vision to bring premium coaching to local students.
2016
Growing Family
Crossed 100 students. Added dedicated Science and Maths faculty. Introduced weekly tests.
2018
JEE & NEET Wing
Launched specialized JEE and NEET batches. First JEE qualifier from Vertex Education.
2020
Smart Board Learning
Installed digital smart panels across all classrooms. Visual, interactive learning became standard.
2022
ICSE Dedicated Batch
Launched separate exclusive ICSE batches with specialist faculty — first in the Chandkheda area.
Today
500+ Students · 95% Success
Still growing, still personal, still genuinely Vertex.
