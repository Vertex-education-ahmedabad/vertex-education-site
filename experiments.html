📹 How to Add Your Own Experiment Videos — 4 Simple Steps

1
Record the experiment
Use your phone to record in the classroom or lab. Landscape mode recommended.
2
Upload to YouTube
YouTube → Upload → Set visibility to Unlisted (only people with link can see).
3
Copy the Video ID
From youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID — copy the ID part after ?v=
4
Paste in this file
Find dQw4w9WgXcQ in experiments.html → replace with your copied ID. Done!
